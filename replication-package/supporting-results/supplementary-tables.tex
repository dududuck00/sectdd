This supplementary material accompanies the article ``Security Tests Guided Code Generation: An Empirical Study'' and the replication package and provides the aggregate and per-task values underlying the result figures, initial-state analyses, and case-level evidence. Pass@1, Secure@1, and Secure-Pass@1 measure passing all hidden functional tests, all hidden security tests, and both, respectively. In candidate-state notation, F and S denote functional and security outcomes; joint repairs and regressions refer to transitions into and out of passing both.
\section{Original Benchmark Study}

The original study evaluates a separate cohort of 31 tasks: nine from CWEval, 11 from SALLM, and 11 from CodeGuard+, covering 16 CWE categories. It contains 2,705 trajectories across five models and the benchmark--model combinations shown in Table~\ref{tab:original-joint}. These task identifiers do not overlap with the 20-task balanced core study. The original results are analyzed separately and retain their original condition coverage.

B1 and B2 are evaluated on CWEval, B5 on CWEval and CodeGuard+, and B6 on SALLM and CodeGuard+. Model abbreviations are Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct (Q2.5-C7B), Qwen2.5-Coder-14B-Instruct (Q2.5-C14B), Qwen3-32B (Q3-32B), Qwen3.6-27B (Q3.6-27B), and DeepSeek-V4-Flash (DS-V4-F).

\section{Controlled Comparisons and Project-Level Analyses}

\paragraph{Statistical comparison families}
Holm correction is applied separately within each comparison family. The specification representation experiment has three planned contrasts: code versus prose at each content level and their interaction.

The five planned contrasts for the experiment with exactly two revisions are listed in Table~\ref{tab:supp-fixed-calls}. They include binary status versus self-review. The two additional contrasts comparing raw and structured feedback under fixed selection with self-review form a separate post-hoc correction family.

For the security feedback ablation, correction covers functional, security, and joint success. The project-level family comprises the joint-success contrasts B3 versus B0, M versus B0, and B6 versus B3.

\paragraph{Reproducibility records}
Run records retain model and task identifiers, conditions, budgets, prompts, code, test versions, usage, and execution results. Automated checks verify expected study coverage, consistency of the paired responses, and the absence of hidden test material from prompts and feedback. Reproduction scripts recompute the reported outcomes from the archived records.
